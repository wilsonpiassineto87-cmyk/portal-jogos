Privacy And Cookie Policy

The Short Version

Jogatina does not use cookies, does not show adverts, does not use analytics or tracking services and does not collect personal data. All the games and the daily content run inside your own browser.

Information Stored On Your Device

To remember your preferences and progress, the site saves a few values in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device:

  • visitsCounter – how many times you have opened the homepage.
  • gamePlays – roughly how many games you have played (used for achievements).
  • dressupCounter – how many dress-up games you have opened.
  • gfxPerformance – a quick graphics speed test, used to choose animations.
  • niceColour1, niceColour2 – your chosen background colours.
  • frivExtraTimer – the day you unlocked the extra games.
  • randomAssignment – a random number used to vary small details of the page.
  • arcadeBest_*, pixelPainter – your best game scores and your pixel art.
  • dailyVotes, feedbackLog – your thumbs up/down votes and feedback messages.

You can delete all of this at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Children's Privacy

Because nothing is collected, there is no personal information about children (or anyone else) for us to store or share.
