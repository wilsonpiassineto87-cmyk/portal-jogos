Help Accessing The Site

To play the games simply visit our homepage ...you can use just about any device with an up-to-date browser. Click a game to open it, wait for the play button to appear, then click it to start. Your game opens in full screen; use the arrow in the top left corner to go back to the menu.

If a page looks broken, reload it. Make sure you are not using a translator, proxy or an old browser, and try turning off any extensions that change web pages.

Some games need a keyboard and mouse; others are made for touch screens. The little icons on each game's loading screen show which controls it uses.

About Jogatina

Jogatina is a collection of small, fast and friendly browser games. There are no downloads, no in-app purchases and no ads interrupting your game. Every game was built for this site, and runs entirely in your browser.

Besides the games, the Jogatina Daily section has something new every day: a joke, a riddle, a fact, a famous quote, a song, pictures, a reaction test, a bedtime story and more.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Do I need an account?

No. Just open a game and play.

Where are my scores saved?

Best scores, your chosen background colour and a few counters (visits and games played) are stored in your own browser using local storage. Nothing is sent to us. Clearing your browser data resets them.

How do I search for a game?

Click the magnifying glass in the top right corner of the homepage. You can browse by first letter or by category.

Can I change the colours?

Yes! Click the small coloured tab under the search button. Every click picks new colours; every tenth click goes back to the original ones.

Are there any secrets?

Maybe. Regular players sometimes unlock achievements... and clicking things more than once is always worth a try.

Jogatina Fakes & Forgeries

The real Jogatina never asks you to install anything, never asks for passwords or payment, and never interrupts a game with an advert. If a site does any of these things while using our name, it is not us.
